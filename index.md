## Project Showcase

### Impact of Education on Annual Salary and Weekly Working Hours
**Research Question:** 
How do different educational attainments correlate with earning potential and work commitment among individuals under the age of 65?

**Visualization & Findings:**
![Salary and Hours by Education](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jiahaofang77-droid/data-science-project/main/a_subplot.png)

*As shown in the visualization, individuals with a "Bachelor's degree or higher" demonstrate the widest range of annual salaries, with a noticeably higher upper bound compared to other groups.*
